Choosing Representation

Questions to Ask a Penngrove Realtor or Real Estate Agent

Whether you are interviewing me or another real estate professional, these are useful questions to ask before choosing representation.

How much experience do you have specifically in Penngrove?

Ask about actual transactions and familiarity with the community, not simply whether Penngrove appears on an agent's service-area list.

Have you handled properties similar to mine?

This can be especially important for acreage, country property, additional units, shops, wells, septic systems or commercial buildings.

How would you determine the property's value?

A strong answer should go beyond an automated estimate and explain how the agent would account for land, improvements, condition and comparable properties.

How will you market the property?

Sellers should understand the plan for photography, presentation, online marketing, buyer outreach and how the property's unique features will be communicated.

Who will I actually communicate with?

Some consumers prefer working directly with one broker while others prefer a team. Neither structure is automatically better, but you should know what to expect before signing an agreement.
