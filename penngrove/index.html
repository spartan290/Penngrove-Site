Penngrove Real Estate Guide

Penngrove is a small Sonoma County community with a real estate market that is very different from many of the surrounding cities. Properties here can range from homes near downtown Penngrove to larger country properties with acreage, workshops, barns, wells, septic systems and other features that require local knowledge.

This resource was created by Aaron Loewenthal, Broker and owner of Penngrove Real Estate, to provide buyers, sellers and property owners with practical information about real estate in Penngrove.

Buying a Home in Penngrove

Buying in Penngrove often involves considerations that are not as common with conventional subdivision homes. Depending on the property, buyers may need to evaluate acreage, wells, septic systems, outbuildings, access, zoning and potential future uses in addition to the house itself.

Selling a Home in Penngrove

Selling a Penngrove property requires understanding what makes the property valuable to the right buyer. Location, land, usable acreage, improvements, shops, additional structures and development potential can all affect how a property should be positioned and marketed.

Country Property

Country properties are an important part of the Penngrove market. These properties can be considerably more complicated than a typical residential sale because the land and infrastructure can be just as important as the residence.
